HELLO 👋

I’m Gulsheen Kaur Bhatia, a UX designer at Tapestry and a graduate of Wilfrid Laurier University’s User Experience Design program. For the past eight months, I’ve worked on digital experiences across the Coach and Kate Spade brands, using design to solve real problems and make technology easier for people to use.

I was born in the US, raised in Delhi, and moved to Canada four years ago. Adjusting to each new place taught me adaptability and resilience, and I bring both to my design process and my work with cross-functional teams.

I’m creative, organized, and collaborative, and I’m driven to build meaningful experiences through user research, wireframing, and prototyping. Outside of work, I love experimenting in the kitchen, where my creativity finds a different outlet.

I’m excited to keep learning and to collaborate with fellow designers on user-friendly solutions that make an impact.
